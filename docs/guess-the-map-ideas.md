# Guess the map ideas

Each post starts with an unlabeled thematic map. The answer can be on the next slide or saved for the comments, depending on the post.

## Easy

- NATO members.
- Countries that use the euro.
- Countries that drive on the left.
- US states with no income tax.
- World population by country.
- US state population.
- Countries with land borders with only one other country.
- States won by the same presidential candidate in the last three elections.

## Medium

- GDP per capita by country.
- Median age by country.
- Foreign-born population share by US state.
- Counties with the highest share of residents over 65.
- Countries that have hosted the Olympics.
- Countries with McDonald's locations.
- US counties with a coastline.
- States by share of electricity from wind.

## Hard

- African American population share by US county.
- Broadband access by US county.
- Home insurance nonrenewals by county if sourceable.
- Earthquake hazard by region.
- Coffee production by country.
- Remittances as a share of GDP.
- US counties by ancestry group plurality.
- Places with the fastest home price growth.

## Expert

- Writing system by country.
- Koppen climate category plurality.
- Tectonic plate boundaries without labels.
- Population-weighted elevation by country.
- Shipping chokepoints or ports by cargo volume.
- Countries by dominant crop acreage.
- US counties by dominant religion if sourceable.
- Watersheds or river basins without labels.

## Entry fields

- Difficulty.
- Question text.
- Clue text.
- Answer text.
- Source line.
- Join key.

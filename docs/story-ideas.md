# Story ideas

This backlog is for one-off posts tied to the news, a seasonal peg or a recurring curiosity.

## Politics

- Presidential approval over time, with current president against recent presidents.
- Generic ballot swings since the last midterm.
- State approval gaps between governors, senators or presidential candidates.
- Presidential margin by county compared with demographic change.
- Voter registration shifts in states with party registration.
- Congressional retirements by party and cycle.

## Economy

- Mortgage rates since 1971, with a payment example on the answer slide.
- Consumer sentiment vs. inflation.
- Gas prices by state before a holiday weekend.
- Wage growth vs. inflation.
- Unemployment claims during shutdowns, strikes or disasters.
- Credit card debt per household.

## Weather and climate

- Billion-dollar weather disasters by year and region.
- Heat alerts or heat index records during a heat wave.
- Hurricane tracks that made landfall in the same region.
- Tornado warnings by month.
- Rainfall departures from normal after a flood event.
- Snow drought or snowpack by basin.

## Culture and names

- SSA baby names that rose after a movie, song, athlete or politician.
- Names that peaked in a specific decade.
- Regional spread of a name by state.
- Words or phrases rising in Google Trends.
- Most common birthdays.
- Age patterns for a given first name.

## Geography

- County population change since 2020.
- State-by-state EV adoption or charging access.
- County-level commute time.
- Where new homes are being built.
- Median age by county.
- State migration flows.

## Sports

- Team payroll vs. wins.
- Franchise value by league.
- Stadium age by team.
- Longest playoff droughts.
- Travel distance by team schedule.
- Attendance changes after a championship season.

## Quick filters

Use these tests before building:

- Is there one clear question the slide answers?
- Can the data update or be rerun?
- Is the source public enough to cite?
- Will the chart still read on a phone?
- Is the visual more interesting than the table behind it?
